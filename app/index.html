    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    Built from the official trophy descriptions only: story chapters and named missions first, then character and stranger strands, then collectibles and exploration, then activities and combat, then money and cleanup. The platinum unlocks when the other 54 are earned. Counts below come from PSNProfiles' tracked targets (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garage properties).

    Roadmap order is our reading of the official descriptions, not a claim about the game's internal mission order — that has not yet been checked in the game (it launched 19 Nov 2026).

    Missable watch list — 14 trophies to do on time

    What “missable” means here: the trophy is tied to a specific story mission, a character strand's final mission, or a choice. Finish it while that mission or choice is in front of you, and keep a save before the finale. Whether mission replay covers a missed one has not yet been checked in the game — so treat these as do-it-now, not do-it-later.

      Chapter trophies (Chapters 1–4) are not flagged missable: they unlock from story progress alone. Collectibles, activities, combat and money trophies are all repeatable in free roam as far as the official descriptions show.
